What is this?

Where Scouts meet Blocks

ScoutCraft is a small, friendly Minecraft server built by Scouts, for Scouts — celebrating the spirit of the World Scout Jamboree 2027. Whether you're building base camps or going on adventures, there's always something to do.

⚜️

Scout Spirit

Rooted in the values of scouting — teamwork, respect, and adventure — brought into the blocky world.

🌍

Jamboree 2027

Themed around the World Scout Jamboree 2027, connecting Scouts across the globe through Minecraft.

🤝

Small & Friendly

Close to 12 players in a tight-knit community. Everyone knows everyone — no strangers here.

🎉

Occasional Events

From build contests to group expeditions, we run events to keep things fun and bring the crew together.

The Community

Come hang out with us

We're self-hosted and proud of it — two friends running a server for a crew of Scouts who love Minecraft. It's casual, it's fun, and the door is always open.

Drop into the Discord to chat, coordinate events, or just share what you've been building. We're always around.
